About Telegram Downloader

Telegram Downloader for Windows 11

Version 1.0.0 (Production 24/7 Edition)

A production-ready Windows 11 24/7 background media downloader and monitor service. Built with FastAPI, Telethon, SQLite WAL mode, and native Windows Service architecture.

Features & Architecture

  • ⚡ 24/7 Windows Background Service: Operates continuously without a logged-in user.
  • 🛡️ Safe Storage & Atomic Writes: Downloads stream to .part files, verify integrity, and rename atomically.
  • 🔄 Crash & Reboot Recovery: Interrupted downloads automatically resume on startup without losing queue state.
  • 🌐 Tailscale & LAN Ready: Accessible across your home network and remote VPN mesh.
  • 🍿 Jellyfin Media Sync: Automated library scans upon completion of video and music files.
